## April 1, 2024 DONE

It's funny that after all these years, the first thing u sent me was: "Je savais pas que c'était toi 😂😭" I almost didn't recognize your account either. We spent years in the same middle and high school, at MLK in Bussy-Saint-Georges, barely talking. And then one random night after 1 am, we found ourselves talking as if all those silent years had quietly folded into a few minutes.

I answered with the dumbest possible "wsh tu dates" trying to joke around, and u immediately called me out: "Depuis quand tu parles comme une racaille ?" Fair enough. I deserved it. It made me laugh because it broke the ice instantly. No awkward introductions, no pretending we remembered every detail about each other.

U told me u were still studying, but really, u were out seeing the world. U said u were almost doing a world tour, and I won't lie, I was jealous. While u have been collecting countries and memories, I have been spending my days finishing my engineering internship. Different kinds of journeys, I guess. Then I told u about my plans for my trip to Korea and Japan, something I have been working so hard for. Your answer made me smile more than I expected: "Ramène-moi." Such a simple thing to say, but it carried that easy humor that had settled over the whole conversation. Like we were just two old classmates catching up, even though the truth is we never really knew each other that well back then. U didn't even considered me as your friend lol, but I did.

The strange part is how natural it all felt. Years disappeared without explanation. Maybe that's what happens sometimes. U don't need a shared history of constant conversations. Sometimes all u need is one random late-night message and the willingness to reply. Still, I'm glad u texted. Who would have thought that one random message after 1 am would make an ordinary night memorable?
